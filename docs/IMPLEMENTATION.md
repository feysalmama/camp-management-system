# Holy Sites Operations

## Selected stack

- React, Vite, TypeScript for the application.
- Tailwind CSS and reusable React components for the interface.
- Supabase PostgreSQL, Auth, private Storage, and Edge Functions.
- Cloudflare Pages for the frontend; GitHub for source and deployments.

## Implemented modules

1. Foundation: app scaffold, environment configuration, database migrations.
2. Registration: country selection, company creation, immediate signup sessions, membership approval.
3. Organization: company users, roles, camps, service providers, issue types.
4. Tool 9: incident creation, assignments, actions, comments, attachments, status history.
5. Tool 10: supervisor escalation, situation report drafts, approval, PDF output.
6. Dashboard, audit history, notifications, deployment and access-control verification.
7. Privileged platform/country administration, movements, camp clearance and reconciliation.
8. Optional provider-backed report emails and Stripe billing functions (disabled until configured).

## Access rules

Company is the tenant. Every operational record belongs to one company. Database Row Level Security must enforce access independently of the UI.

Creating a company makes its creator company admin. Selecting an existing company requests membership: it never grants immediate access or admin rights. Platform and country admins are provisioned through trusted administration, never registration fields.

Roles: company admin, supervisor, camp manager, service provider, viewer. Platform and country administration are separate privileged scopes. Country admins receive supervisor-level access to companies in their country; super admins receive company-admin access across the platform. An account has one active or pending company membership. Privileged users can switch managed workspaces.

## Reporting workflow

Tool 9 statuses: draft, open, in progress, escalated, resolved, closed. Supervisors approve closure. Escalation creates one linked Tool 10 report; later updates revise that report with audit history. Escalation and report creation must run in one database transaction.

Tool 10 includes summary, impact, current situation, immediate actions, resources required, recommendations, author, approver and timestamps.

Never expose a Supabase service-role key in browser code. Attachments use private buckets and authorized downloads. Audit events are generated by trusted database logic.

## Reporting assumptions

- Obtain official Tool 9 and Tool 10 templates to verify fields and PDF layout.
- Country and company remain separate entities. The company creation form suggests the selected country as its default name; countries do not automatically create empty company workspaces.
- Each incident has at most one linked situation report. Draft/submitted situation reports can be edited; approved reports and closed incidents are locked.
- In-app incident notifications go to active company members; situation-report changes notify supervisors and company admins. Notifications refresh on user actions or the explicit refresh control.
- Camp managers and service providers can create/update company incidents and operational records. Only supervisors/company admins escalate, close, approve, or manage operational catalogs. Company admins approve memberships.
- Related camps/providers/issue types cannot be deleted while referenced; deactivate them instead. Incidents and reports are retained for accountability.
- Severity uses four fixed levels (low, medium, high, critical), while issue categories are managed through issue-type CRUD.

## Free pilot scope

In-app notifications and printable reports need no external messaging service. Email and Stripe functions need provider secrets and live verification. SMS, WhatsApp and video uploads are not enabled. Free hosting is subject to provider quotas and Supabase inactivity pausing; assess paid hosting and backups before live operational use. Operational lists load up to 5,000 rows per module, notifications 100, and audit history 250; move to server-side pagination before expanding beyond a pilot.
